The Ouija

Short edition, sourced from the author's document.

The Ouija

        It was a late summer night, somewhere around 10 PM; the boys had been anticipating this
night for an entire week. And it was the perfect night – as it was the end of summer, there was a
cool breeze, the night had an eerie aura with a quiet stillness that made it clear most kids were
starting to settle in at home to welcome the school year. Not these boys; there were the younger
ones, Anthony (16), David (15), and Josh (13). Then there were the older ones, Carmelo (20),
and Harry (21). Carmelo asked the group, “You guys ready?”

        A week earlier, it had all started with a random conversation that took a spin around the
topic of horror movies, and the coming of Fall, with it – Halloween. Carmelo had asked
everyone if they had ever played the Ouija board; of everyone in the group, he had only played
with Harry before. None of the others had, but they were intrigued.

        “A Luigi board?” Josh asks, “what’s that?”
        “Not a Luigi board, an Ooo-wee-gee board” He responds, as if to emphasize strange
pronunciation, unlike any common word in the English dictionary. “It’s a board that lets you talk
to ghosts; you ask them whatever you like and they answer you.”
        “Can you see them?” Anthony chimed in.
        “No dummy, you can’t see them. It’s a board!” Carmelo continues explaining the game,
if you would call it that. “It has all the letters of the alphabet, it has yes, no and maybe and it
moves a glass around the board. You have to read the letters it stops at.”
        “Ooo, I want to try it” says David, the little daredevil always volunteered for the more
extreme activities.
        “We should!” Carmelo smiles at Harry and gives him a wink.
        “But when? And where? It’s already late.” Anthony states.
        “It would have to be next weekend, it’s over at my house and I’m not going over there to
get it now.” Carmelo pauses, “maybe we can play in the abandoned building” he says with a
grin, pointing toward the abandoned, boarded up townhouse, two townhouses away.

         Carmelo couldn’t hide his excitement, he is the alternative goth in the group, dresses in
all black, wears a lot of spikes from his jewelry to his hair; moved by anything horror, he spends
a lot of his time sketching his favorite horror characters.

         “We can’t go in there” says Harry, “plus they have that big dog in there guarding the
place. We can use my place.”
         “Your place?” Carmelo asks, “the cellar?” He grins again.
         “Yeah” Harry replies.
         “Let’s do it, that cellar is pretty much an abandoned house” Carmelo jokes.
         Harry has been working on building a den in his cellar, the ceilings are short, it’s dark,
and still needs a lot of work. The perfect setting for the Ouija board.
         “I don’t know” says Josh with the look of worry upon his face “this sounds scary.”
         “Don’t worry Josh, if it gets to be too much, we’ll just leave” says Anthony, comforting
his little brother.
         “Great!” says Carmelo, “This is gonna be fun” he says with a large smile across his face,
showing half excitement and full creepy to the rest of the group.

       -   One Week Later       -

        “You guys ready?” Carmelo asks as he pulls the Ouija board out of his black backpack
and lays it on Harry’s dusty table,
        “Almost, David should be getting here any minute now.” Anthony answered.
        “That's cool,” says Harry, someone upstairs will let him in. “Melo remember the last time
we played?
        Carmelo answers “Of course, I can’t wait to see what’s out there tonight.” He says with a
wink back at Harry.
        Harry smiles “You know the deal.”
        The wooden stairs creak as the footsteps thump on their way down. David turns at the
base of the stairway, swatting away spiderwebs he must have felt hit his face on the way down.
“Wassup guys!?”
        Unsynchronized greetings come from the group of friends, Harry adds on “Who let you
in?”
        “Gladys” he replies, Harry's little sister.
        “Cool. Is that everybody?” Harry asks the group as he glances around.
        Carmelo replies, “I think so.”
        Harry cuts off the light bulb that only faintly illuminated the cellar and lights a candle; as
Carmelo undid the box containing the Ouija board.
        Carmelo, leaning over the board, calls everyone in to learn the rules of the game. “Bring
it in guys – In Ouija, everyone must hold the glass together and we start the spin as we ask the
question using our voice, we will each take a turn – clockwise. After the question, the ghost
should take over and answer us using the board. Sounds easy enough?”
        He glances up, noticing the orange glow upon their faces, Josh staring wide eyed and
petrified.
        Anthony says, “Yep, I think we got it.”
        “First, we have to call to it and get it to respond to us. I'll start us out.” Carmelo begins,
“Hi, my name is Melo, I'm looking to make contact with a spiritual being. Anybody out there?”
        The group of five, each with their arms stretched over the table toward the center– held
the glass up, a few inches over the board, and spun it in circular motion. “When you guys feel the
ghost start moving the piece stop forcing it, just gently keep your fingers on the piece and let the
ghost take over” Carmelo says softly, tossing a smirk at Harry and Rich, before repeating the
question, “I am looking to make contact with a spiritual being, anybody out there?”
        The circular motion continues until the piece begins to shift; the younger boys gasp, not
breaking contact with the instrument, as it leans and hovers over the word:

       Ghost: “YES”
       Anthony: “How many of you are there?”
       Ghost: “JUST ME”
       Josh: “Can you see us?”
       Ghost: “YES – I AM IN THE ROOM”
       *Josh looks around the room frightenedly*
       Harry: “Where in the room are you?”
       Ghost: “STANDING OVER YOU”

       *The younger boys gasp again*
       David: “How long have you been here?”
       Ghost: “105 YEARS”
       Carmelo: “Can you give a sign to show us that you’re in here?”
       Ghost: “ WHAT WOULD Y-“

        The silence is broken by a tumbling sound in the background, as the piece shakes in the
boys’ hand breaking free from their grasp, falling onto the board.
        “What the heck was that!?” Says David turning to look behind him toward the sound.
        “The cat” says Harry, “It must have jumped on the dresser again, I’m always telling it not
to.” Harry recently bought a cat, a little over a month ago, to keep mice at bay while he cleans up
the cellar. He named it The Cat, he never planned to bond with it, it’s more like mouse patrol.
        The boys turn their attention back toward the game. Glass piece back in spinning motion.

       Carmelo: “Did you cause that tumbling sound?”
       Ghost: “NO”
       Anthony: “Can you prove that you’re here or are you completely worthless?”
       Ghost: “A JOKER, I CAN READ YOUR FATE”
       Josh: “What do you mean?”
       Ghost: “ANTHONY WILL DIE WITHIN FIVE YEARS”

        Josh turns to his older brother “he knows your name.” Anthony sucking his teeth gives
off a soft nervous laugh.

     Harry: “How about a sign, by making something happen or move in this room?”
     *The candlelight waves and flickers*
     Ghost: “I REALLY DO NOT LIKE BEING DISTURBED”
     David: “That was creepy – Can you get us another ghost who wants to play along?”
     Ghost: “NO”
     Carmelo: “So what can you do?”
     Ghost: “WHO IS ANNA?”
     *The boys look at each other, then to David in confusion.*
     Anthony: “Anna is David’s girlfriend. Why?”
     Ghost: “SHE IS VERY DECEI-“

        Before finishing its sentence, the piece gives another shake, breaking away from the
boys’ grasp and on to the board. David’s phone, which was lying on the table, lights up and
begins to vibrate. “It’s Anna” he says as he goes to answer. “Hello?” Her murmurs can be heard
by everyone in the dark, quiet cellar; David signals to his friends he will take the call to the
opposite side of the Cellar.
        The boys decide they will continue without David until he returns, after discussing how
freaky and coincidental that was. Back to the board, the piece in circular motion. Harry
volunteers to pick up where they left off.

       Harry: “Did you know that Anna was going to call?”
       Ghost: “YES”

       “Harry excuses himself, goes upstairs*
       Carmelo: “Did you mean to say Anna is very deceitful?”
       Ghost: “Yes”
       Anthony: “Can you give us another sign of your presence here with us?”
       Ghost: “I SAID NO. CONTINUE AND I WILL GO FOR THE LITTLE ONE”
       Josh: *nervously* “Are you talking about me?”

         The piece breaks from their grasp again as a loud resounding “YES!” echoes over the
cellar, a deep voice. Most of the group jump in shock, Josh filled with fear turns to his brother, “I
don’t want to play anymore.”
         Anthony assures him “Just a little while longer, don’t worry I’m here.”
         Josh insists on the verge of tears “No, I want to go home.”
         David returning from his phone call, “what’s going on here? Everything alright?”
         Carmelo answers “I don’t know, I think we’re going to wrap this up. Everything alright
with you? With Anna?”
         David responds “Yeah. It’s Ok. Kinda odd that she called when she did.”
         Anthony adds in “Yeah that was freaky, anyway we’re heading out.”
         The boys share their goodbyes; the younger ones make their way up the stairs toward the
front door, passing Harry on his way back down.
         With the younger ones away now, the older guys discuss the game. Carmelo starts “Whoa
that was good! Good move on the ‘YES!’ that was powerful!” he reaches over for a high five.
         Harry responds “Yeah. I don’t know man, I felt it coming and thought this is my
moment.” He continues “David’s girl calling when she did was spooky as hell.”
         Carmelo agrees as he says goodbye, leaving Harry to shut down the cellar before heading
upstairs to his room.
         This game was intriguing; as always, I enjoyed watching it. I have gathered enough
information and material to work with. The only hard part is who do I start with – they’re all
game and interesting enough to mess with. Maybe I will start with Rich, for the loud YES! It is
true I really DON’T like to be disturbed. The game is fun; the noises are not! I will enjoy this, I
will disturb them, the way they have disturbed me! Let the HAUNTING begin!

A Journey Through the Rapture

Into the Ark



Here comes the tsunami

Before the flood arrives

Into the ark…

       All you movies and games

       And all entertainment that helps me stay sane

       All the days filled with random

       Cars blasting music, like its their favorite anthem

       Strangers interacting and smiling
       That feeling you get when you go outside stylin’

       The natural sense of surprise

       The light shine upon your face as you watch the sunrise

       All of your hopes and dreams

       The hope that it’s true, everything isn’t what it seems

Here comes the tsunami

Before the flood arrives

Into the ark
All that makes you feel alive.


                        Noah’s Ark- The Poem


                    I’m going to need a guiding light
                      To guide us through the dark
                     Feels like it was only last night
                      I had a dream about the Ark


                  Yes, the one with the animals in pairs
                     The one that survived the flood
                 There are so many things that we share
                     Down to the DNA in our blood


                     I’m new to this but so are you
                        In fact, I am much older
                    I’ll do anything to get us through
                 And you will always have my shoulder


           So, listen here bears, tigers— and constricting boas
         Make some room in this Ark and welcome my son Noah.


Astral Dreams


I often think that multiple realities run in coexistence
Many realities in which we are present in an alternate experience
A different reality, a different dimension
But there we are, living there as a different person,
The us we would have been, had we made THAT other choice
The choice where we decided to speak up, where we took a chance
Took that job, went to that school, asked that girl to dance
Every choice we didn’t make, that could have opened new paths
Occurred in these realities, and we are there presenting in new masks
Sometimes we communicate with these other realms, it would seem
As we are given glimpses of these other lives in dreams
It has been more than fifteen years since I gave up my aspirations
And thirty years since I crawled into a play space station
But when I was young, I wanted to be an astronaut
Until high school, my grades said I ought to not
But recently I dreamed that I was aboard a spaceship
A dream so vivid I can still taste it
The liquid meal with my crew I didn’t know would be my last
Some were dressed up, some dressed down, in gym apparel
Some in military uniform, commanders in battle
The vibe is chill, like the night’s winding down
Except for the uniformed people pacing around
I watched them; they seemed to be on high alert
I decided to turn toward my cabin, but he caught me first
“General, great job, today was a success, I’m turning in”
“Get dressed? Why? There are no tasks set for the rest of the evening”
“Oh, simulations? Sure, I’ll meet you in the ready room”
Seems urgent I guess I’d be best to get ready soon
I put on my astronaut gear and met with the general


I felt a bit anxious; I didn’t know what I was headed to
In the ready room, with little conversation
He directed me as we walked together toward the docking station
“Wait in here” he says, “standby for orders”
We’re in the middle of nowhere, suddenly I need water
First, I heard the buzzer, then the decompression at the hatch
I turned toward the exit and tried to reach for the latch
But it’s too late, like a vacuum, the deep consumed me
Out of the ship, no O2 tank, no crew, just me
It all happened so fast, this isn’t right
But it must have been the longest minute in my life
I wrestled with the little oxygen in my suit until “CRACK!” There goes my mask
My consciousness fades away until I wake up with a gasp
I interpreted the dream as a look into a parallel realm of existence
Where on this very night, in that reality, in this very instance
I took my last breath, had I made THAT choice, had I gone down that line
I thanked God that we are always where we are meant to be,
and in this reality, he has granted me more time.


                                  Heaven II

              You wake up to notice all your windows are open

                  The clock isn’t ticking, perhaps it’s broken

             Look outside, blue skies, white clouds, soft as cotton

         You walk to the window to an array of colorful buildings and

                Wow! Look at that! More clouds at the bottom

                       No streets, no sidewalks, no cars

                      No schools, no hospitals, no bars!

              “Look out, here I come! Hi, it’s nice to meet you”

              “Can you see me standing here at your window?”

                  “You must be wondering how am I flying”

                    “You can too, if I’m lying, I’m dying”

                      “Hah! Too late! Don’t you get it?”

              Come outside, so I can show you around heaven”
